## 2. Prior literature on the central claim

Central claim: the SM fails every Pauli condition. The fermion sign is statistics, not antiparticles. No SM-only structure relates the sums. The background supertraces are beta_λ and Veltman, whose zeros are known near-criticality facts.

- Pauli conditions and their scheme dependence: Pauli 1951; Visser 2016/2018 (cutoff, zeta, dim reg; Lorentz invariance iff the three polynomial conditions; eq. 17 finite part; sec. 10.1 says the SM needs BSM states). Akhmedov 2002 and Koksma-Prokopec 2011 give the Lorentz-invariant (ρ = -p) treatment.
- The SM fails them: Alberghi et al. 2008 (JETP Lett. 88, 705) prove the SM cannot cancel without new bosons and a new massive fermion. Kamenshchik, Starobinsky, Tronconi, Vacca, Venturi, arXiv:1604.02371 repeat it with the 750 GeV resonance. Visser sec. 10.1.
- SM dim-reg vacuum energy numbers: Koksma-Prokopec 2011 (top, W, Z, Higgs; "56 orders of magnitude"); Martin, Comptes Rendus Physique 13, 566 (2012), arXiv:1205.3365, eqs. (515)-(516) and Fig. 5 (ρ_vac ≈ -2×10^8 GeV^4 at his chosen mu; plots ρ vs mu including the sign change); Ossola, Sirlin, EPJC 31, 165 (2003), hep-ph/0305050. The line's 10^53-10^55 ρ_Λ range is an update of these with PDG 2024 masses.
- Running of the cc counterterm ∝ Str m^4: standard. Shapiro-Solà (hep-th/0012227, hep-ph/0305279) give the m^4/16π^2 beta function with decoupling. Visser eqs. 45-46 give the RG flow of the Pauli sums.
- Str M^2(φ)/φ^2 = Veltman coefficient: stated explicitly in Masina, Quiros, PRD 88, 093003 (2013), arXiv:1308.1242, eqs. (9)-(11), with weights 1 (h), 3 (G), 6 (W), 3 (Z), -12 (t). This is the line's "Pauli-quadratic in background = Veltman" point, including the Goldstone weight.
- Str M^4(φ)/φ^4 ↔ beta_λ: this is the one-loop Coleman-Weinberg RG-consistency condition (μ-independence of V_tree + V_1-loop at large φ). I checked the line's identity algebraically: 2 Str M^4(φ)/φ^4 = 24λ^2 + (3/8)(2g^4 + (g^2+g'^2)^2) - 6y_t^4, which equals 16π^2 beta_λ minus the anomalous-dimension term λ(12y_t^2 - 9g^2 - 3g'^2) in the V ⊃ λ|H|^4 normalization. Correct, and textbook. I found no paper that frames it as "Pauli's quartic condition in a Higgs background = beta_λ", but the content is not new.
- Fermion zero-point sign from statistics, hole theory = symmetrized second quantization: textbook.

Established or ruled out? Ruled out. SM-only Pauli cancellation is excluded in Alberghi et al. 2008 and in Visser 2018 sec. 10.1. The Lorentz-invariant SM estimate is in Koksma-Prokopec and Martin.

## 3. Gate item 6 verdict

Restatement of known work. WORK.md 3d already says "largely a restatement". The literature check makes that stronger: two of the four claimed additions are also in print.

Claimed additions, reassessed:
- (i) Dirac-sea derivation showing the sign is statistics: textbook. Not new.
- (ii) Scheme-by-scheme table of which Pauli conditions survive, with SM numbers: the scheme analysis is Visser secs. 4-6 and Koksma-Prokopec. The SM numbers update Koksma-Prokopec and Martin with PDG 2024 and the full spectrum. Minor numerical update.
- (iii) mu* = 83.6 GeV as a regulator artifact: correct and harmless, but trivial. Martin Fig. 5 already shows the sign change of the mu-dependent one-loop term.
- (iv) Str M^4(φ)/φ^4 = 8π^2 beta_λ(non-anomalous), and Pauli-quadratic vs Veltman (Higgs weight 1 vs 3 from Goldstones): the Veltman half is Masina-Quiros eqs. 9-11. The beta_λ half is standard CW RG consistency. The specific packaging ("Pauli's quartic sum rule in a background is beta_λ, so it inherits Higgs near-criticality") was not found stated this way. It is a relabeling of a known identity and produces no new constraint, as the line itself concludes.

Also adds: m_t = 87.5 GeV (Str m^4 = 0) and 81.2 GeV (Str m^2 = 0) as single-parameter thresholds. Alberghi et al. eqs. (4)-(6) give the equivalent top-vs-W/Z numbers in units of m_t, so this is a re-expression.

No new derivation or constraint. "Nothing new physically" is the honest verdict.
