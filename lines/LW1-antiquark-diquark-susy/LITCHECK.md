## 2. References added in cycle 3 (all verified on INSPIRE / Crossref)

- Fleming & Mehen, PRD 73, 034502 (2006), hep-ph/0509313. Primary: rederive the 3/4 relation (their eq. 2) from NRQCD. The leading corrections come from 4-heavy-quark + ultrasoft-B operators suppressed by α_s², plus operators suppressed by α_s(m_Q)² v². No numerical correction is given ("future publication"). They say the relation holds "at the 30% level" for charm. Their "experimental value of 78 MeV" is based on SELEX candidates that LHCb never confirmed, so that comparison is obsolete.

## 3. Novelty verdict (gate item 6)

**Restatement. The core framing is in the literature. Only a narrow addition is new.**

1. "The meson→baryon map replaces the antiquark (fermion, 3̄) with a 3̄ diquark (boson)". Lichtenberg (hep-ph/9912280) says this almost verbatim: replacing the antiquark (a fermion) by a 3̄ diquark (a boson) is "a supersymmetric transformation of a meson into a baryon". The same idea is in Catto-Gürsey 1985 and Dosch-de Téramond-Brodsky 2015. **Known.**
2. "The SM's *only* realised boson-fermion symmetry". **WRONG as stated.** Nuclear dynamical supersymmetry (Iachello 1980; Metz et al. 1999, experimental evidence in the ¹⁹⁵Pt/¹⁹⁶Au region) is another approximate Bose-Fermi symmetry realised in SM matter, also emergent from QCD + QED. The line must drop "only" or say "the only one acting on the SM's elementary charge carriers (quark ↔ diquark)". [established that nuclear SUSY is claimed experimentally; its status is a model-level symmetry like LFHQCD]
3. Heavy quark-diquark prediction for Ξ_cc*, Ξ_bb*: **Known.** Savage-Wise 1990, BVR 2005 (120 ± 40, 34 ± 4 MeV), Fleming-Mehen 2006, Brown et al. 2014 Table XX (lattice ratios). The line's 106.5 / 34.0 MeV is the BVR D- and B-based LO number. It is not a new prediction.
4. "This hadronic SUSY cannot address SUSY #1 because the bosonic partner is composite at Λ_QCD". **Not found stated explicitly** in any source checked (Lichtenberg, Dosch et al., Brodsky et al., BVR, FM). Dosch et al. mention the hierarchy motivation of SUSY and that their SUSY is "not based on a supersymmetric quantum field theory", but they draw no hierarchy conclusion. The argument is an immediate consequence of standard naturalness reasoning: a symmetry that emerges below a compositeness scale cannot control UV-sensitive corrections above it. So it is new only as an explicit statement. It is not new physics. [conjecture that no paper states it; search was INSPIRE + web, not exhaustive]
5. The independent LFHQCD fit showing the s = 1 (ρ-Δ) family works at a few percent and the s = 0 (π-N) family is off by 10-27%: the pion's lack of a partner and the b₁/N mismatch are visible in Brodsky et al.'s own figures. The quantified split is a minor new check.

Gate item 6: fails as a standalone result. What would count as new: (a) the explicit negative statement for #1 written as a short theorem-like argument with the compositeness scale quantified, and (b) the cycle-3 c4 lattice-calibrated Ξ_bb* number (30 ± 4 MeV, below). This is consistent with overseer ruling R5: fold into the S1 synthesis line or add a "What is new" section limited to (a) and (b).
