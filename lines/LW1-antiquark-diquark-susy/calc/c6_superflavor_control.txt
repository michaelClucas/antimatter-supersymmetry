   Verdict [established for these models]: the AQM (no superflavor) predicts r_SF = 0; the two DGG fits
   (no superflavor tie) predict r_SF = -13.2 and +14.2 MeV. The observed +4.0 +- 1.6
   MeV is reproduced by the AQM with no superflavor, so it is NOT evidence for superflavor. The data do show
   a pattern: pairs where the s quark sits next to a heavy source (D_s*, B_s, Omega_cc) have |r| <~ 14 MeV,
   pairs where it shares a light diquark have |r| = 10-80 MeV. That pattern is heavy-quark symmetry
   (suppressed light-heavy spin coupling), of which superflavor is one instance; it does not single it out.
