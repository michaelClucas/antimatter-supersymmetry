3. Explicit SUSY breaking by quark masses (paper eqs. 23-25): Delta M^2 depends on the constituents,
   so each hadron gets its own shift. Then E0 = m_pi^2 != 0 and the pairing splits; the zero mode is
   'protected' only in the chiral limit, which is the same statement as chiral symmetry (GMOR).
