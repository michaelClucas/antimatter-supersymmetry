# LW1-antiquark-diquark-susy

Wildcard explorer, cycle 1.

## Idea

An approximate boson-fermion (super)symmetry is seen in Standard Model hadron spectra: mesons (q qbar) and baryons (q [qq]) line up as approximate supermultiplets. The map that relates them replaces an **antiquark** (fermion, colour 3bar) with a **diquark** (boson, colour 3bar, since antisym(3x3) = 3bar). (Cycle 6 wording, convention-independent: every supermultiplet pairs a matter object with an antimatter object in the same colour representation, e.g. Qbar <-> QQ or Q <-> Qbar Qbar, so a world without antiquarks has none. It is not the SM's only realised boson-fermion symmetry: nuclear dynamical SUSY is another, but that one involves no antimatter.) [established as a structural fact; references below]

Two levels:

1. **Heavy-quark superflavor** (Savage & Wise, PLB 248, 177 (1990), DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(90)90035-5; Brambilla, Vairo, Roesch, PRD 72, 034021 (2005), arXiv:hep-ph/0506065). In the m_Q -> infinity limit of QCD, a heavy antiquark Qbar and a compact heavy diquark (QQ)_3bar are interchangeable static colour sources. This follows from the QCD Lagrangian via NRQCD/pNRQCD. [established]
2. **Light-hadron supersymmetry** from light-front holographic QCD (Brodsky, de Teramond, Dosch, Lorce, PLB 759, 171 (2016), arXiv:1604.06746; Dosch, de Teramond, Brodsky, PRD 92, 074010 (2015), arXiv:1504.05112; historical: Miyazawa, Prog. Theor. Phys. 36, 1266 (1966), DOI 10.1143/PTP.36.1266). This is a semiclassical model of QCD, not a derivation from the Lagrangian. [established that the model exists; its equivalence to QCD is [conjecture]]

## SUSY results targeted

- **#6 (null results)**: the SM already contains a realised, approximate boson-fermion symmetry whose "superpartners" are observed hadrons. Nothing new to find. [established for the hadronic sector]
- **#1 (hierarchy)**: negative result. An emergent SUSY of composite states at scale ~Lambda_QCD cannot cancel UV-sensitive Higgs mass corrections. [conjecture, argued in WORK.md]
- Not targeted: #2, #3, #4, #5 (argued irrelevant in WORK.md).

## Falsifiable output

Superflavor predicts M(Xi_cc*++) - M(Xi_cc++) = (3/4)[M(D*0) - M(D0)] = 106.5 MeV at leading order (lattice QCD: ~83 MeV). Not yet measured. LHCb can test it. Cycle 8: see the CLAIM at the top of WORK.md (107 +- 32 MeV, still unmeasured as of 2026-11-25: LHCb has observed Xi_cc+ and Omega_cc+ but no J=3/2 state; Xi_bb* - Xi_bb = 30 +- 4 MeV; the #1 negative rests on compositeness, the Higgs being a Q-singlet, and HLS, and does not use antimatter).

## Scope

QCD only. Diquarks here are colour-3bar qq correlations inside hadrons, not new fields. No BSM content.
