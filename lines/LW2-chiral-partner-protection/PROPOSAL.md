# LW2: chiral-partner protection (wildcard, cycle 6)

**Targets:** SUSY #1 (scalar mass protection), with side remarks on #4 and #6.

**Idea.** The SM already contains one scalar whose gauge-loop quadratic divergence is cancelled exactly by partner states built from matter and antimatter: the charged pion. Its photon-loop mass shift m(π+)² − m(π0)² is finite because the vector (ρ-like) and axial (a1-like) quark-antiquark spectral functions obey the two Weinberg sum rules (DGMLY 1967). This is a constructive SM match to the SUSY #1 structure: partner states with the same spin and opposite chirality-parity play the role superpartners play, the sum rules play the role of the supertrace conditions, and the chiral splitting m_a1² − m_ρ² plays the role of soft breaking. [established physics; the explicit SUSY dictionary is this line's framing]

**Question.** Does the same mechanism act on the Higgs inside the pure SM?

**Plan.**
1. Derive the DGMLY result from the sum rules and show it cancels both Λ² and ln Λ, compared with soft SUSY (top/stop), which cancels only Λ² (calc/c1).
2. Treat h and G0 as the SM's own chiral partners (0+ scalar and 0− pseudoscalar t̄t channels under SO(4) ≅ SU(2)L×SU(2)R); compute which part of the Higgs mass the partner structure protects (calc/c2).
3. Test whether h can be an SM-content q̄q or t̄t composite, which would be needed for the pion mechanism to protect μ² (calc/c2, calc/c3).

**Expected outcome.** Partial match plus a sharp obstruction; if protecting μ² needs new strong dynamics, the line stops there (BSM).
