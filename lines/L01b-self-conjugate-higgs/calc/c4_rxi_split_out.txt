Part 2. Gauge-invariant groupings (any U(1)_em-preserving linear gauge, any xi_W, xi_Z, xi_gamma)
  (a) by physical particle, Goldstones absorbed (W = 3 pol. incl. eaten G+-; Z incl. eaten G0):
        C_W = 3/2 g^2 + 2 lam,   C_Z = 3/4(g^2+g'^2) + lam,   C_h = 3 lam,   C_gamma = 0,   C_t = -6 y_t^2
      => 'charged pairs' (W+W-) = C_W, 'self-conjugate' (Z, h, gamma) = C_Z + C_h: identical to the line's
         Landau-gauge split, because each sector sum is xi-independent separately.
  (b) by coupling structure: transverse-vector part (g^2 terms) vs scalar-curvature part (lam terms):
        both xi-independent sector by sector (the mixed block + ghosts sum to m_G^2 in every family).
  (c) NOT gauge invariant: 'W seagull alone', 'Goldstone alone', 'V-G bubble', 'ghosts'.
  Limit of validity: a gauge fixing that breaks U(1)_em (e.g. axial n.A with charged-neutral mixing) makes
  'charged sector' ill-defined field-by-field; the grouping by physical charge eigenstates is still fixed by
  the SU(2)xU(1) group theory (W1,W2 vs W3/B; G1,G2 vs G3) and the total is invariant.
