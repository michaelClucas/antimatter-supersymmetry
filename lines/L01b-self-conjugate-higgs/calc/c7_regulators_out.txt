Sector coefficients with transverse multiplicity d-1 (xi drops out):
  W = (d*g**2 - g**2 + 4*lam)/2
  Z+h = (d*g**2 + d*gp**2 - g**2 - gp**2 + 16*lam)/4
  d=4 (cutoff, PV, DRED): W = 3*g**2/2 + 2*lam, Z+h = 3*g**2/4 + 3*gp**2/4 + 4*lam
  d=2 pole of DREG     : W = g**2/2 + 2*lam, Z+h = g**2/4 + gp**2/4 + 4*lam

Cutoff and PV agree because both weight every physical dof of a 4D field equally; DREG's d=2 pole does not.
