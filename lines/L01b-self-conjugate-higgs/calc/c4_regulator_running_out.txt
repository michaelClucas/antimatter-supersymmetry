Part A. Per-species weight of the quadratically sensitive term in three regulators
  cutoff:  I(M^2) = int^Lambda d^4k/(2pi)^4 1/(k^2+M^2);  coefficient of Lambda^2 = 1/(16*pi**2)  (M-independent)
  Pauli-Villars (2 regulators, Lambda, 2 Lambda): leading term = Lambda^2 x -log(2)/(6*pi**2)  (M-independent: d/dM2 = 0)
  dim. reg.: A0 = Gamma(1-d/2)(M^2)^(d/2-1)/(4pi)^(d/2); residue at d = 2: -1/(2*pi)  (M-independent)
  => weight of species i in d^2V/dphi^2 is n_i * d^2 M_i^2/dphi^2 x (universal constant) in all three.
     Ratios of the C_i (hence R2, the 34% / 79%) are identical in cutoff, PV and DR(d=2 pole). [established]
     Caveat: only at one loop; the 2-loop Lambda^2 coefficient is scheme dependent (Einhorn-Jones, PRD 46,
     5206 (1992)), and at d = 4 in DR the term is absent.

Part B. STr M^4 gauge dependence: Fermi-gauge mixed block, coefficient of 1/k^4 in ln det
  mixed (A_L,G) block: sum m^4 = -2*M**2*mG2*xi + mG2**2  -> xi term proportional to m_G^2 = lam(phi^2 - v^2)
  so d^2/dphi^2 STr M^4 at phi = v is xi-dependent (contains 2 m_G^2' M^2' ~ xi lam g^2 v^2), while the lam^0 and
  lam^2 parts of the phi^4 coefficient are not. R4 below keeps only those. [established; Nielsen NPB 101, 173 (1975)]
