## 2. Prior literature on the central claim

- **Nucleon-antinucleon freeze-out in a symmetric universe** ("annihilation catastrophe"): Zeldovich (1965, not checked here); Chiu, PRL 17, 712 (1966), 10.1103/PhysRevLett.17.712 (verified); Steigman, ARAA 14, 339 (1976), 10.1146/annurev.aa.14.090176.002011 (verified); Kolb & Turner (1990); Steigman-Scherrer 1801.10059. The line's solver reproduces the textbook number.
- **Matter-antimatter symmetric cosmologies excluded**: Steigman 1976; Cohen-De Rujula-Glashow 1998; antistar limits Dupourque et al. 2021.
- **Antibaryons count as baryons in CMB/BBN**: standard; follows from C invariance of QED/QCD. No separate paper needed.
- **Antibaryonic / baryon-symmetric dark matter**: Farrar-Zaharijas 2006 (BSM CP violation; H version excluded); Hylogenesis, Davoudiasl-Morrissey-Sigurdson-Tulin, PRL 105, 211304 (2010), 1008.2399 (hidden-sector antibaryonic DM, BSM); Blinov et al. 1206.3304; Gorbunov-Pakhlov, "Antibaryonic dark matter", 1303.5914. All need new fields. This is the asymmetric-DM literature.
- **SM-only dark matter surveys**: Gross et al. 1803.10242, "Dark Matter in the Standard Model?", is the direct precedent: it examines the sexaquark and SM-Higgs PBHs and rejects both. The line's sections 3(d) and 3(b) repeat it.
- **Sexaquark**: Farrar 2017-2023 for; Kolb-Turner 2019, Gross et al. 2018, McDermott et al. 2019, BaBar 2019 against. Live debate, not settled; Farrar disputes the abundance argument.
- **Quark nuggets**: Witten 1984, Alcock-Farhi 1985, Zhitnitsky 2003, and the crossover-compatible reappraisal 2404.12094 (see #11).
- **SM "R-parity" is trivial**: definitional; R = (-1)^{3(B-L)+2s} was built to be +1 on SM fields (Farrar-Fayet 1978, ref not checked here). Not a result.
