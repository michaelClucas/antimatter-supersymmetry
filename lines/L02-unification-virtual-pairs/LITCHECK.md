## 2. Prior literature on the central claim

- **SM non-unification** is established since 1991: Amaldi, de Boer, Furstenau, PLB 260, 447 (1991), 10.1016/0370-2693(91)91641-8; Langacker-Luo, PRD 44, 817 (1991), 10.1103/PhysRevD.44.817; Ellis-Kelley-Nanopoulos, PLB 260, 131 (1991), 10.1016/0370-2693(91)90980-5 (all DOIs resolved). 2- and 3-loop SM running to M_Pl with NNLO matching is 1307.3536 itself; the line reproduces it. The "SM needs alpha_s ~ 0.07" statement is the standard way the 1991 papers phrased the failure.
- **Fermions drop out of the one-loop unification test** because complete SU(5) multiplets shift all b_i equally: standard since Georgi-Quinn-Weinberg (1974). The ratio (b2-b3)/(b1-b2) as a one-loop unification test is the "B-test" of Giveon, Hall, Sarid, PLB 271, 138 (1991) (bibliographic record confirmed; definition taken from secondary sources). The line should cite it.
- **Extra Higgs doublets unify the SM couplings**: known. Willenbrock, "Triplicated trinification", hep-ph/0302168 (PLB 561, 130 (2003)) and later 6-Higgs-doublet papers (e.g. arXiv:1709.02366) report unification with ~6 doublets at ~1e14 GeV with fast proton decay. The line's one-loop w_S = 7.45 at 10^13.7 GeV is the same result at lower loop order (one-loop check: n_H = 7.45 does give B = 0.718). Cite these as the known version.
- **Antiparticle "contributions" to running**: no paper found that proposes antiparticle loops change SM running (one targeted search, nothing). Hole-theory vacuum polarisation reproducing covariant QED is the 1934-1936 literature cited (Heisenberg, Weisskopf, Uehling). The only formal case of antiparticle over-counting in a reformulation is NCG fermion doubling/quadrupling (Lizzi et al. 1997; Gracia-Bondia-Iochum-Schucker 1997; resolved by Connes 2006, Barrett 2006).
- **Spectral action unification**: CCM themselves state the couplings do not meet exactly (hep-th/0610241 Sec. 5.1). The m_H ~ 170 GeV failure and the sigma-field repair (1208.1030) are well known.

## 3. Gate item 6 verdict

Restatement of known work. Nothing new as physics.
- Non-unification numbers: known since 1991, reproduced at 3 loops with PDG-2024 inputs. Values update the inputs but not the conclusion.
- Antiparticle counting: textbook QFT (one field = particle + antiparticle; hole theory = covariant QED). No literature proposes the opposite, so there is nothing to refute in print.
- Fermion-independence of the one-loop test and the extra-Higgs fix: known (GQW 1974; Giveon-Hall-Sarid 1991; Willenbrock 2003).
- Spectral action failure: stated by CCM and by the 2008 Tevatron exclusion.

What the line adds: (a) an independent 3-loop runner validated against 1307.3536 to 4 digits, with PDG-2024 numbers (useful tooling, not a result); (b) an explicit state-by-state counting script and residue check that answer the project's own question cleanly. Neither is publishable as new physics. The line should cite Amaldi et al., Langacker-Luo, Giveon-Hall-Sarid and Willenbrock so its "established" tags point at the actual sources.
