### Findings

1. **major. PROPOSAL.md:9 (item 2), WORK.md:46.** The claim that fermion antimatter contributes zero to one-loop non-unification "under ANY weighting of antiparticle loops" is false. It holds only for a weight that is uniform across each complete SU(5) multiplet (5-bar + 10) of a generation. Counterexample: weight antiquark states only. Per generation the antiquark half-contribution is Delta b = (11/30, 1/2, 2/3), which is not proportional to (1,1,1), so w_F no longer drops out of the B-ratio. Species-dependent weights (quarks vs leptons, 5-bar vs 10, colour-dependent) could in principle be tuned toward unification. The no-go still holds because such weights are not SM-equivalent (item 1) and are killed empirically, since the R-ratio and Delta alpha_lep test quark and lepton counting separately. Fix: replace "ANY weighting" with "any weighting uniform within a generation". Add one sentence and a `counting.py` case showing that non-uniform weights are excluded by item 1 and by the separate quark (R-ratio) and lepton (Delta alpha_lep) data, not by the SU(5) argument.

2. **minor (bordering major). PROPOSAL.md:8 (item 1) vs WORK.md:48.** Item 1 says "any other weighting changes measured quantities ... by factors of order 2". The empirical checks (`empirical_counting.py`) only test fermion-loop weights. The two reweightings that would actually unify, w_S = 7.45 and w_V = 0.134, get no quantitative empirical kill. For w_V it is a one-liner: b_3 = -11(0.134) + 4 = +2.53 > 0, so QCD loses asymptotic freedom, which contradicts measured alpha_s running. Fix: add that number to `counting.py`. For w_S, either give a measured observable it breaks or say plainly that it is excluded only by definition (not the SM field content, A1). The current WORK text ("contradicts ... the Slavnov-Taylor identities") is asserted, not computed.

### Support and falsifiability

The central claim (SM antimatter, counted in any SM-equivalent way, cannot change gauge running toward unification) is supported. The algebraic part is textbook (Gross-Wilczek/Politzer, Georgi-Quinn-Weinberg, CPT). The numerical non-unification (spread 3.18 in alpha^-1, required alpha_s ~0.074) is reproduced and matches the standard literature. The line makes no new prediction, which is expected for a no-go. It does contain real consistency checks against data: Delta alpha_lep, the R-ratio, alpha_s running, and the RG validation against 1307.3536. Finding 2 asks to extend those checks to the gauge and scalar reweightings. No numerology is proposed as a mechanism: k = 1.339 and w_S = 7.45 are reported as what would be needed, so H1 passes. No BSM states enter a mechanism. nu_R, U(1)_X, sigma and the spectral-action boundary condition are all explicitly excluded.
