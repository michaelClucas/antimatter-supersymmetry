# L03: SM antimatter as dark matter

**Target SUSY result:** #3, a stable dark matter candidate (SUSY: LSP stabilised by R-parity, R = (-1)^{3(B-L)+2s}).

**Question.** Can any Standard-Model antimatter configuration (relic antibaryons/antileptons, antimatter in compact objects, antiquark nuggets, SM bound states such as the hypothetical sexaquark and its antiparticle, anything else) constitute or mimic the cold dark matter, Omega_c h^2 = 0.120 (E1)? And does any SM conserved quantity involving antimatter play the role R-parity plays in SUSY?

**Expected outcome.** A negative result, stated as tightly as possible:

1. Thermal freeze-out of nucleon-antinucleon annihilation gives Omega h^2 ~ 10^-11 in a symmetric universe and essentially zero antinucleons with the observed asymmetry. Committed Boltzmann solver.
2. Any antibaryonic matter that is ionised at recombination counts as baryons in the CMB (Thomson scattering on e+ = on e-), so it is bounded by Omega_b, not available for Omega_c.
3. The complete list of stable SM states, sorted by the conserved charges Q, B, L, (-1)^F, contains exactly one neutral, non-baryonic species (the relic neutrinos/antineutrinos), and it is hot and 100x too little.
4. SM "R-parity" is identically +1 on all SM states, so the SUSY stabilisation mechanism has no SM content.
5. The only SM-field-content loophole is the hypothetical uuddss sexaquark (B = 2): its existence is unobserved (scope question for overseer), and it is disfavoured by abundance, nuclear-stability and neutron-star arguments plus BaBar.

**Scope.** SM fields and observed facts only. The sexaquark is flagged as "SM field content, unobserved state" and treated only conditionally. Axion quark nuggets, PBHs from non-SM primordial perturbations, and BSM baryogenesis are out of scope and noted only to explain why they are excluded.

**Falsifiable content.** If a deeply bound uuddss state with m_S < 2.05 GeV were found (e.g. in Upsilon decays, below the BaBar limit, or on the lattice at physical quark masses), item 5 changes and the line must be reopened. Detection of cosmic antihelium at the AMS-02 candidate level (E9) would not change items 1-4 (it bounds antimatter at far below Omega_b).
