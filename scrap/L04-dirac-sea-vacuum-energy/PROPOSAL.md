# L04: Dirac sea and vacuum energy (Pauli supertrace conditions)

**Target SUSY result:** #5, exact cancellation of boson and fermion zero-point energies when SUSY is unbroken (and SUSY's failure on the cosmological constant once broken).

**Idea.** In hole theory the fermion zero-point energy is the energy of the filled Dirac sea, which is negative. This is the only place where "antimatter" enters vacuum energy. SUSY cancels the boson zero-point energy against that negative contribution, multiplet by multiplet. Pauli (1951) wrote the conditions a general spectrum must satisfy for the same cancellation without SUSY:

- Str 1 = Σ(−1)^F g_i = 0 (quartic divergence)
- Str m² = 0 (quadratic)
- Str m⁴ = 0 (logarithmic)
- Str m⁴ ln m² = 0 (finite part)

**Question.** Does the SM spectrum, or any SM-only structure (rep content, anomaly cancellation, Dirac sea counting, an SM-equivalent reformulation such as the NCG spectral action), satisfy or enforce these conditions?

**Plan.**
1. Derive the zero-point energy of a scalar d.o.f. and of a Dirac field (hole-theory sea) with a hard cutoff and in dim-reg; show which Pauli conditions survive a Lorentz-invariant treatment.
2. Evaluate all four sums for the SM with measured masses; check running masses; compare to ρ_Λ.
3. Check SM-only structures for a hidden relation.
4. Expected outcome: rigorous negative result with numbers.

**Scope.** SM fields only. SUSY used only as a comparison target. NCG used only in its SM-equivalent sector (A3).
