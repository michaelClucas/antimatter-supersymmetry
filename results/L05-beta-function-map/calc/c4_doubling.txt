   THEOREM (two loops, gauge part, any gauge group G = prod G_i, any matter): let T have Weyl fermions F,
   complex scalars S_c, real scalars S_r. Let Phi*(T) = vector multiplets for each G_i + chiral multiplets
   F u S_r u {s, s' : s in S_c} with s' any rep having the same (S_i, C_j) as s (e.g. conj(s)). Then
   b_ij[Phi*(T)] = 2 b_ij[T] for all i != j and for every abelian i = j. Proof: per-field weights of section 0;
   the delta_ij pieces are proportional to C(G_i), zero for U(1).
   Minimal supersymmetrization equals Phi* only when the complex scalars have no anomaly-free vector-like pairing
   among themselves and each one's fermion partner needs a NEW conjugate partner. The one-doublet SM is such a case.
   Counterexamples (c6_scope.py): complex scalar in a real rep (3,8) -> 26/25; pseudoreal (2,8,0) -> 8/7;
   vector-like scalar pair -> 1; 2HDM parent -> MSSM gives 3/2 and 199/104. The factor 2 belongs to the parent choice.
